# Lycian Odyssey Audio Dataset (v2.0.0)

Dataset 2 of the **TRAIL – Lycian Odyssey** Digital Rural–Urban Enabler, developed by ATAM Technology, Artemis Outdoor and Antalya SKAL within the RURBANIVE Open Call (Horizon Europe, project 101136597). It contains the location-triggered cultural audio stories played in the TRAIL application along the Lycian Way. The locations and story texts are published in the *Lycian Way Trail, Segment, Point and Cultural Storytelling Dataset* (Dataset 1).

Release date: 7 November 2026. This release replaces the WAV files of v1.0.0 with MP3 files named by story identifier and adds metadata and transcripts.

## Contents

| File | Content | Records |
|---|---|---|
| `audio/<story_id>.mp3` | Narrated cultural stories, MP3 128 kbit/s | 35 |
| `audio_metadata.csv` | Story identifier, linked location, title, language, file, duration, size and licence | 37 |
| `transcripts.csv` | Full transcript of each story | 37 |

Stories 33 and 222 have a transcript but no published recording. Language: English. `story_id` and `location_id` link to `stories.csv` and `points_of_interest.csv` in Dataset 1.
